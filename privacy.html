Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who uses it

Only the owner of GTA6 Clipper uses it. No other users sign in.

What is stored

  • Sign-in tokens for the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts, kept only on the owner's computer.
  • Public live-stream chat messages and short recordings, used to find and cut clips, deleted automatically after about 30 minutes.

What is not done

  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • No data from other TikTok users is collected through the TikTok API.

Removal

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in their TikTok, Google and Meta account settings, which ends all access.
